About Me

I was born in Los Angeles and raised in San José, California. I began my college education in San Jose before moving to Los Banos, where I completed my Associate's degree in Studio Arts at Merced College. I am currently studying for a B.A. in Studio Practice and plan to continue my education with the goal of pursuing a career in Art Therapy.

My work explores different themes through digital media, web design, 3D modeling, miniature worldbuilding, and mixed media art. Through my artwork, I hope to bring creative expression into the field of Art Therapy to help individuals with mental health challenges or disabilities explore artistic mediums as tools for communication, self-discovery, and healing.
