About Me

I was born in Los Angeles and raised in San José, California. I began my college education in San Jose before moving to Los Banos, where I completed my Associate's degree In Studio Arts at Merced college. I am currently Studying for B.A in Studio Practice and plan to continue my education with the goal of pursuing a career in Art Therapy. My work explores different themes through digital media,web design, 3D modeling, miniature worldbuilding, mixed media art.Through My artwork , I hope bring all this knowledge of creative expression into the field of Art therapy in hopes of helping individuals with mental health challenges or disabilities explore different artistic mediums as tools for communication, self discovery and healing.
